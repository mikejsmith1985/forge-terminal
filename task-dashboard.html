🎯 How to Use

  1. Press Ctrl+, to open Settings
  2. Go to Shell tab
  3. Find "Default Theme for New Tabs"
  4. Choose your preference:
    • Auto-Cycle All Themes (original)
    • Auto-Cycle (Dark Only) ⭐ NEW
    • Auto-Cycle (Light Only) ⭐ NEW
    • Or pick a specific theme
  5. New tabs will follow your preference!
